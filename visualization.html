⚡ FEM Suture — 3D Stress Visualization

─ FEM Suture Tool — Method & Assumptions

What this tool does

Quasi-static, large-deformation Finite Element Analysis of a soft-tissue suture pad. Four sutures are tightened one at a time (Phases 1–4). At each phase the suture thread pulls the wound edges together; the tool records the evolving von Mises stress field and wound-closure displacement across all 32 output frames.

Contact between the wound faces is enforced with an Augmented Lagrangian Method (ALM), preventing interpenetration as the wound closes. The nonlinear system is solved with a damped Newton–Raphson scheme with automatic bisection fallback.

Material model

Compressible Neo-Hookean hyperelastic solid — appropriate for large-strain, nearly-incompressible soft tissue. The strain-energy density is Ψ = μ/2 (I₁−3)−μ ln J + λ/2 (ln J)², where μ and λ are the Lamé parameters derived from E and ν.
